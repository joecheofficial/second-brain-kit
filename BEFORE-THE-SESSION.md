# Before the session (10 minutes, a day or two ahead)

Exports are emailed to you, and can take from a few minutes to a day to
arrive. Request them now so they are ready.

## 1. Request your exports

**ChatGPT:** open ChatGPT → your name or picture → **Settings** → **Data
controls** → **Export data** → **Export**. You get an email with a download
link. The link expires after about 24 hours, so download the zip as soon as
the email arrives.

**Claude:** open claude.ai → your name → **Settings** → **Privacy** → **Export
data**. You get an email with a download link. Download it when it arrives.

Leave the zips in your **Downloads** folder; you do not need to unzip them.
Using a work or team account? Exports may be switched off by your admin. Use
what you have; the session works with one export, or none.

## 2. Check two things on your laptop

Open a terminal (on a Mac: Spotlight → "Terminal") and run:

```sh
node --version
git --version
```

- `node` should print `v24.something` (or `v22.18` and later). Anything else, or
  "command not found", is fine: the setup will install the right one, but it
  helps to know in advance.
- If `git` asks you to install developer tools on a Mac, say **Install** and
  let it finish (it can take 10 to 20 minutes). Doing that now saves session time.

## 3. Think about what you want it to find

Two or three questions you wish your assistant could answer today, such as
"what did I decide about my pricing?", or "what did Sam send me about the
venue?". You will use them to test it at the end.

## Bring

Your laptop, charged, with Claude Code or Codex signed in, and your exports.
